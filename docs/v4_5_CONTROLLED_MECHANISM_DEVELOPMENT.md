# v4.5 Controlled-Mechanism Development Contract

## Objective

Close the previously failed controlled tangent-geometry denominator on fresh seeds while preserving the original scientific mechanism and every historical result. This phase is engineering development, not confirmation evidence.

## Immutable scientific objects

- benchmark, truth, observation design and loss weights: P2 locked controlled problem;
- source pair: `sin3x_sin2t` and `sin4x_constantt`;
- alpha grid: `[0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1]`;
- nominal gamma alpha: `1e-10`;
- eta definition, monotonic tolerance `1e-8`, Spearman threshold `0.9` and planned monotonic count `8/10`;
- explicit/matrix-free agreement threshold `1e-6` and solver residual threshold `1e-8`.

Historical P2 remains `SG-1 FAIL`. V4.5 cannot relabel or reaggregate it.

## Development isolation

- engineering: seeds `85--87`;
- held-out development: seeds `88--89`, inactive until executable freeze;
- untouched confirmation: seeds `90--99`, inactive until a separate lock and authorization.

Center engineering may use only exact state-local-minimum status, unchanged first-order stationarity, validation-only state RMSE, finite residuals, solver validity and runtime. Eta, monotonicity, Spearman correlation and plot appearance are forbidden selection inputs. All attempted candidates and failures must be retained.

## Development gates

Engineering may freeze one deterministic center workflow only if all three engineering seeds have a valid center and all five alpha evaluations per seed pass parameter-curvature solver and explicit/matrix-free agreement. Held-out development must then pass the same binding chain on both seeds. Development eta values may be recorded fail-soft but cannot authorize or select the executable.

Only after held-out acceptance may a separate confirmation protocol be drafted. Confirmation success rules cannot be modified using seeds `90--99`.
